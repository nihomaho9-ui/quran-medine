What we do not collect

There is no account. We do not take your name, email, phone, location, contacts, photos, or device id. There are no ads, analytics, or trackers. Data is not sold or shared.

Language, text size, the tafsir and translation you pick, bookmarks, and downloaded files stay on this device only. They are not sent to a server.

Internet is used only when you download a tafsir or a translation, over HTTPS. You can delete that file in Settings.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. The content is the Quran, with no ads, and it is suitable for all ages.
